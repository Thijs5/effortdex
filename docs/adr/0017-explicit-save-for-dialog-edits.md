# 17. Explicit Save for Nature/IVs; single-tap actions consolidate into an Items popup

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Every field in `components/caught-pokemon-detail.js`'s dialogs used to call a `store.*` mutator directly on `click`/`change`/`blur`, no separate commit step — a deliberate choice, documented inline at several sites: the IVs dialog's contents were "no toggle of any kind gates this... reaching it via the 'More' menu is the opt-in"; the training-item grid's "each button applies its item immediately on click... there's no separate 'None' option or save step"; vitamins "instantly add EVs to one stat without battling — a quick way to top off a stat." The reasoning was that these are small, cheaply-reversible edits (`store.deleteHistoryEntry` undoes anything that logs an event; re-clicking a toggle or a mutually-exclusive item swaps/clears it) so a Save step would add friction without a real safety benefit.

The Level popup (`.level-up-dialog`) broke from that pattern first, for a different reason: a level change can cascade into evolution eligibility, and a stat reading logged at a since-corrected level leaves a stale snapshot behind feeding `possibleIvsFromReadings` — a level shouldn't be live the instant it's typed. In review, "preview locally, commit only on an explicit Save" was judged better for a specific subset of the *other* fields too — not because they share Level's cascading-consequences problem, but because they're the kind of field a stray blur or scroll-triggered `change` can silently commit with nothing but a transient status line as evidence. Two fields ended up in that group: **Nature** and **the IVs dialog's per-stat grid**. Both share the same trait that settled it: neither has a History event of its own to cheaply undo (ADR 0006 — "attributes with no history of their own [nickname, nature, held item] stay plain mutable state on purpose"), so a wrong pick has no built-in safety net the way a mis-clicked vitamin does.

Everything else that used to live in the old "Training & EVs" dialog — Vitamins, Wings, EV-reducing berries, Pokérus, Exp. Share, and the held-item/Macho Brace pick — stays instant, on purpose: each is a single-tap, repeatable-or-swappable action, and every one of them *does* log (or directly represents) a History event, so a mistake is a re-click or a History-entry delete away from fixed. Forcing a Save button in front of "tap Calcium" would add friction to the single most repetitive action in the app for no corresponding safety gain.

Once Nature and Level both moved out to their own popups (each following the same "the header badge is the trigger" shape), the old dialog had nothing training-specific left to justify its own identity — it was Nature plus a grab-bag of everything else. So the "everything else" consolidated into one **Items** popup instead (also off a header badge), and the old dialog was retired entirely. What it was really keeping alive at that point was Release, which is a `confirm()`-gated destructive action with no fields of its own — it moved directly into the "More" menu as a menu item, no dialog wrapper needed.

## Decision

1. **Nature** gets its own `.nature-dialog`, opened from a header badge (`.nature-btn` — reused from the old inline "Adamant Slowpoke" text, now a real `<button>`; shows "Set nature" when unset, so there's always something to tap). It previews locally (`_openNatureDialog` seeds the `<select>` from the entry) and commits only via its own `.nature-dialog-save-btn` ("Save"); closing any other way (✕, Esc, backdrop) discards the pending pick.
2. **The IV grid** (IVs dialog) works the same way: `_openIvsDialog()` seeds `_pendingIvs` from the entry, the grid's inputs write into that pending object instead of calling `store.setIv`, and `.iv-dialog-save-btn` ("Save") applies every stat that actually changed. The IV *calculator*'s "Log reading" button and its result chips stay immediate — already a deliberate one-click apply, out of scope — but a chip click updates `_pendingIvs` for that stat *before* calling `store.setIv` (not after: the store call's save synchronously fires the store's `'change'` event, which re-renders the grid from `_pendingIvs` immediately — updating pending afterward is one render too late).
3. **Nickname stays instant** — it's a card-level field, not dialog-scoped (always editable directly in the title row), so there's no Save button for it to defer to.
4. **Vitamins, Wings, EV-reducing berries, Pokérus, Exp. Share, and the held-item/Macho Brace pick all live in one `.item-dialog` ("Items"), opened from the header's item badge (`.held-item-btn`), and all stay instant.** This badge is never hidden, even on a party whose generation has no held-item mechanic at all (pre-Gen III) — Vitamins/Pokérus/Exp. Share aren't gated on held-item support, so the badge remains the one entry point to all of them regardless of generation; it falls back to a generic "Items" label instead of a misleading "No item" in that case.
5. **The old "Training & EVs" dialog is gone.** Its "More" menu entry is gone with it; the menu is now IVs / Competitive / **Release** — Release moved directly into the menu as a `confirm()`-gated action (`_releasePokemon()`), styled as a `.more-menu-item--danger` entry, with no dialog of its own (it never had fields to defer).
6. **Already-gated actions are unaffected**: Evolve/Undo (behind a native `confirm()`) and the IV calculator's "Log reading" (already its own explicit action) needed no change.

## Consequences

- No more silent instant mutations for the two fields most likely to be a genuine, hard-to-undo mistake (a wrong Nature pick, a fat-fingered IV) — a single "this is a draft until Save" mental model for those two, matching the Level popup.
- Four header badges now open popups (Level, Nature, Items, and — unchanged — none for Competitive/IVs, which stay menu-routed): a consistent "the badge is the trigger" shape across the card, at the cost of more surface area to scan than one combined dialog had.
- The Items popup is a genuine grab-bag (a held-item slot, two toggles, and three feed grids, none sharing a mechanic beyond "instant and forgiving to undo") — a deliberate trade of conceptual tidiness for keeping every low-stakes, high-frequency action a single tap away, none of it gated behind Save.
- Retiring "Training & EVs" removed the two-column wide-dialog layout it needed once Wings/Berries/Exp. Share had made it long; nothing left (Nature alone, then nothing) needed that treatment, so every dialog in this component is now the same compact-card shape.
