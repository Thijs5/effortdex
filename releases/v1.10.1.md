## What changed

Finishes the IndexedDB move from v1.10.0. Logging a battle, using a vitamin, levelling up — every roster change — no longer rewrites the whole roster into `localStorage` on top of saving it to IndexedDB. IndexedDB is the source of truth now; `localStorage` keeps a periodic snapshot (taken when you close or background the tab, and on a slow timer) purely as a fallback.

No visible change in normal use. If a change ever fails to reach storage, a one-off notice now says so on the next launch instead of the roster silently missing it.

## Technical improvements

- `Store#_save()` (IndexedDB path): mirrors the change to the object stores as before, but only writes a tiny `effortdex:rev` marker to `localStorage` instead of the full `effortdex:state` blob. The full blob is now written by `Store#checkpoint()` — fired from `lib/shell.js` on `pagehide`, on `visibilitychange` to hidden, on a 60 s interval, and once at the end of `Store#init()`.
- `Store#init()` reconciles three revisions — the object stores, the `effortdex:state` checkpoint, and the `effortdex:rev` marker — adopting the rows when current, healing them from the checkpoint when a mirror was lost, and firing a `save-gap` event (surfaced as a dismissible banner) when a change post-dating the last checkpoint never persisted. It then realigns the marker so the notice doesn't repeat.
- Without IndexedDB, `_save()` still writes the full blob on every mutation and `checkpoint()` is a no-op — unchanged behaviour for that path.
- The `#storage-warning` banner now carries two distinct messages: the sticky "storage is full, not saving" call to action, and the past-tense, auto-dismissing "a recent change may not have been saved" notice.
- Coverage for the checkpoint/marker model and the `save-gap` path; 351 unit + 119 e2e tests.
