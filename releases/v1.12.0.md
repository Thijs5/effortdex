## What changed

**Effortdex has a new look.** The skeuomorphic Game Boy skin — olive LCD-green wash, the poké-red billboard header with its pokéball seam, the dark footer slab, the segmented "LCD" bars — is replaced by "Field Guide": a warm-neutral trainer's-reference look. Same app, same navigation, same data; every screen restyled.

- **New palette and type.** Bone paper, graphite ink, one swappable poké-red accent, meters that turn gold at the cap. Bricolage Grotesque / Public Sans / JetBrains Mono. The dark theme is a warm night version of the same.
- **The app fills the window.** No more bordered "device" card — header and footer run edge to edge, content sits in a centred column. The header carries the wordmark and, while you're in a party, that party's name.
- **One action bar per screen, always reachable.** A slim bar with the back link and the screen's single primary action; it pins as you scroll and tightens once the page moves, while the app header and footer scroll away.
- **Roster cards** carry a faint diagonal wash of the Pokémon's type(s), the sprite sits on the card with a soft shadow, and the EV total reads next to its bar. Nicknamed Pokémon show only the nickname.
- **The detail page is type-themed** — a faint full-page tint of the primary type, solid type badges under the name, a larger framed sprite, EV bars that lean toward the type. "Log a battle" moved into the action bar; "More" is a compact ⋯ menu.
- **History** on the detail page is always expanded and scrolls with the page, not in its own box.
- **Dialogs** are consistent everywhere: a full-screen sheet on phones, and on desktop they grow to fit their content instead of scrolling inside a fixed box. Opening one no longer grabs a text field.

No change to your data — parties, roster, history, and Transfer links are all untouched.

## Technical improvements

- The retheme is a `tokens.css` + `lib/design-system.ts` change (ADR 0003 held): ~17 component shadow roots picked up the new palette/type/radius with no edit. Structure — header, per-view action bars, card and detail-page anatomy — lives in `styles.css` and the relevant modules. ADR 0028 records the design and its two rounds of review revisions.
- Species elemental types are now tracked: `lib/pokeapi-client.ts` keeps `data.types` on `DomainPokemon`, the per-species cache key is version-bumped so existing installs re-fetch once online, and `lib/constants.ts` gains `TYPE_COLORS` / `typeLabel()`. Types are read from the API cache at render time, not the event-sourced store; a species cached before this release acquires its types on the next online load.
- Every dialog — the light-DOM party/add-Pokémon/filter dialogs and the six shadow-DOM detail dialogs — follows one `.ds-dialog` contract; `BaseDialog` no longer carries a per-dialog mobile opt-out.
- New `--sprite-drop` token (dark in light mode, a light halo in dark) for lifting a sprite off a surface. The full-page detail wash is driven by a `--page-type` custom property set on `<html>` from a `type-change` event.
- 353 unit + 121 e2e tests.
