## What changed

Fixes two related bugs in the add/battle search for species whose default form isn't named plainly after the species itself — Giratina, Deoxys, Wormadam, Basculin, Minior and similar.

- **Pressing Enter without arrowing to a suggestion now works for these species.** Previously Enter fell through to an exact-name match that could never succeed for them, so the species silently failed to be added.
- **These species no longer vanish from a gen-gated party's search entirely.** The generation-availability check compared PokéAPI's species-level names (e.g. "giratina") directly against the search dropdown's variety-level names (e.g. "giratina-altered") — a mismatch for every one of these species — filtering them all out of any party with a resolvable generation (i.e. almost every party).

## Technical improvements

- `components/organisms/pokemon-search.js`: Enter now picks the top suggestion when nothing's been arrow-selected, falling back to exact-match only when there are no matches at all.
- `lib/species-availability.js` resolves a generation's species-level roots to their real variety names via a new `PokeApiClient#getSpeciesVarieties`, before comparing against the search dropdown's candidate list.
- `e2e/support/pokeapi-mock.js` now mocks `/api/v2/generation/*` (previously unmocked, so the availability check silently failed open and never actually exercised the restriction in any spec) and adds a Giratina fixture to cover the species-to-variety mismatch case directly.
- 352 unit + e2e coverage for both fixes.
