What changed vs. the first pass

  1. 1 — Add a Pokémon is now a filled primary icon button in the roster header, same visual weight as "Log a battle".
  2. 2 — Log a battle moved off the top nav bar into the "Active party" section header, next to Heal, right where it acts.
  3. 3 — Deselecting a chip no longer closes the sheet (the underlying bug was a focus check in the search component that didn't know about slotted content — fixed at the component level, nothing to see visually here besides "it still works after you tap").
  4. 4 — Only Bulbasaur (the lead) is pre-selected by default; Chansey has to be tapped in.
  5. 5 — "Fainted" only appears on the battle-party slot now (Charmander, top). The roster card below no longer repeats it — it already shows "In party".
  6. 6 — Swap (⇆) on each filled slot opens a quick picker of the bench to replace that member in one tap; an empty slot is itself a "bring someone in" tap target. Faint (×) stays a one-tap toggle.
  7. 7 — Battle-party grid is a fixed 3 columns (2 rows of 3), each card now wide enough for a name, level, and its two action buttons — no more tiny 56px icons.
